Customer Journey

The 4-Step Dining Flow

1. Entrance & Unboxing

Guest buys RM 45 ticket at counter and opens a physical box/capsule revealing their room assignment.

2. Room Entry

Escorted into 1 of 3 themed rooms featuring immersive soundscapes, scents, and lighting.

3. Menu Choice

Selects 1 of 2 curated set meals included with the entry price.

4. Add-on Upsell

Servers recommend themed cocktails, desserts, and extra side dishes to boost average spend.

Operations Strategy

Solving Operational Hurdles

FLAW 1

Capacity Overcrowding

What if everyone unboxes the same room?

✔ Live Gacha System & Trade Board: 1 pull per table based on live system capacity + lobby trading board.
FLAW 2

Kitchen Overload

3 completely different cuisines overhead.

✔ Shared Ingredient Prep: Same core chicken/fish/carbs, differentiated by sauces & plating.
FLAW 3

Renovation Costs

High cost to rebuild rooms quarterly.

✔ Modular Ambience: RGB smart lighting, projection mapping & decor accessories swap overnight.
