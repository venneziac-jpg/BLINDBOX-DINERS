Guest Experience

The 4-Step Dining Journey

1. Unbox

Guest pays RM 45 base entry and pulls a branded mystery capsule/box in the lobby.

2. Reveal

Unboxing reveals assignment to 1 of 3 distinct themed dining rooms.

3. Immerse

Escorted into an atmospheric room tailored with music, scent, and curated lighting.

4. Dine & Upsell

Selects 1 of 2 set meals included in entry. Staff pushes high-margin drinks & desserts.

Operations Playbook

Solving Operational Hurdles

Flaw: Table Capacity Bottleneck

Overcrowded popular rooms leaving other rooms empty.

Solution: Group Allocation & Trade Board

1 box pull per group table matched to live digital system. Lobby Trade Board lets diners swap cards.

Flaw: Kitchen Complexity

Cooking 3 different cuisines creates huge kitchen overhead.

Solution: Shared Ingredient Line

Shared core proteins/carbs across all rooms, differentiated by room-specific sauces & plating.

Flaw: High Renovation Costs

Quarterly room rebuilds eat away at profit margins.

Solution: Modular Ambience

Smart lighting, projection mapping, soundscapes, and interchangeable table decor replace physical builds.

Financial Strategy

Unit Economics & Margin Target

Base Set Revenue

  • • Base Set Price: RM 45.00 net per head
  • • Final Bill Target: ~RM 52.20 (applying standard 1.16 multiplier for service charge & taxes)
  • • Target Food Cost: Keep base ingredients under 30% (~RM 13.50) via standardized core prep

Upsell Engine (High Margin)

  • • Themed Signature Drinks: RM 15 - 22 (Mocktails / Cocktails)
  • • Theme Add-on Desserts: RM 12 - 18
  • • Target Avg Spend / Pax: RM 65.00+ (significantly boosting net profitability)
