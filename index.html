The Core Concept

The Mystery Mechanic

Diners purchase a mystery box upon arrival. Opening the box assigns them to 1 of 3 themed dining rooms (e.g., Tokyo Izakaya, Jazz Lounge, Malaysian Kopitiam).

The Pricing & Menu

RM 45 per pax flat entry including a full set meal tailored to that room's theme. Diners choose between 2 set menu options. Themes rotate quarterly.

Operational Realities & Solutions

Flaw 1: Room Capacity Bottleneck

If everyone unboxes the same room, waiting times soar while other rooms sit empty.

Solution: Group Unboxing & Trade Board

1 pull per table based on live room availability. Guests can trade room cards on a lobby board.

Flaw 2: Kitchen Overhead

Cooking 3 totally different cuisines strains prep work, labor, and food waste.

Solution: Shared Ingredient Line

All 3 rooms share core proteins and produce, differentiated primarily by sauces and plating.
