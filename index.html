Customer Journey

The Premium Dining Flow

1. Entrance & Unboxing

Guest pays RM 90 entry at the door and pulls a high-quality physical box revealing their room assignment.

2. Room Entry

Escorted into 1 of 3 themed rooms featuring immersive soundscapes, scents, and intelligent ambient lighting.

3. Curated Menu Choice

Guest selects 1 of 2 premium curated set meals (e.g., Wagyu slices, imported seafood) included in entry.

4. Add-on Upsell

Servers recommend curated cocktails, artisanal desserts, and premium side dishes to boost check averages.

Initial Investment

Est. Fit-Out & CAPEX Breakdown

Utilizing modular technology (RGB lighting, projectors) to minimize hard renovation costs across the 3 rooms.

Modular Tech & Ambience

  • Smart Projection Mapping (3 Rooms) RM 25,000
  • Dynamic RGB Lighting System RM 12,000
  • Zoned Audio / Scent Diffusers RM 8,500
  • Total Tech Fit-Out ~RM 45,500

Furniture & Physical Decor

  • Base Tables/Seating (Est. 45 Pax Total) RM 30,000

*Estimates exclude structural kitchen build-out and HVAC systems.
